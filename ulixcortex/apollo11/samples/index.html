Reading the table

Class is the PSR rock type: A/B basalts, C breccias, D soils, fines, and cores. Most samples lack precise field provenance — documented sampling was cut short — so the collection is best understood by family, not by individual pin. The fuller picture is in the sample inventory, the returned-samples analysis, and the provenance reconstruction.
