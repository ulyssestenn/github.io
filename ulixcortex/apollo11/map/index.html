Interactive photo-map — Tranquility Base

The only direct image of the Apollo 11 hardware as it sits on the Moon — the 2011 LROC NAC frame at ~25 cm/pixel — annotated with what the mission record places at the site. Drag to pan, scroll or use the buttons to zoom, and switch between the close annotated crop and the full orbital frame. The feature key below links each marker to the articles behind it.

Zones, not pins

A per-sample pin map is not possible from the record: documented sampling was cut to about ten minutes, so most samples lack precise field provenance. Only the lander and the two experiment packages are sharp enough to locate by pixel; the sampling areas are approximate, transferred from the Preliminary Science Report’s traverse map. The full method — and a genuine north/south conflict in the documented-samples placement — is in the Tranquility Base photo-map analysis and the discrepancy ledger.
