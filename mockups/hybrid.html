Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

Does Keep Clip require an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for. Open the app and start clipping.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, in a local database you can back up or export at any time.

What can I export?

Markdown, CSV, TXT, RTF, and HTML — for Obsidian, Logseq, or your own pipeline.

Is it really free?

Yes. Core features are free forever. Premium is a one-time purchase, never a subscription.
