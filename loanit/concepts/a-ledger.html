Reminders

A nudge the morning it is due

Set a due date and Loan It can remind you at 9:00 a.m. local time. Dates display the way your device formats them and are stored in a stable format underneath, so exports stay readable anywhere.

Your lending history is nobody else's business

Loan records and photos are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Loan It reaches the network in exactly two places, and you can see both of them.

  • No account to create
  • No ads, no third-party analytics
  • Open Library, only when you scan a barcode
  • Google Play, only to buy or restore Pro
  • Export to CSV or JSON whenever you want
  • Included in Android backup and device transfer
