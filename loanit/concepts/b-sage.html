When it leaves

Write it down once

Item, photo, borrower, and a due date. Pick the borrower through Android's own contact picker — Loan It never reads your whole address book.

While it is out

Find it in a second

Search by item or name. Filter by overdue, due soon, outstanding, or returned. Sort by due date to see what is coming back next.

When it comes home

One tap, and it is closed

Mark it returned from the list, the borrower page, or the details screen. Undo is there if you tapped the wrong row.

Books

Privacy

Your records stay on your phone

Loans and photos live in the app's private storage. There is no account to create and no analytics or tracking SDKs in the app. Loan It touches the network in exactly two places.

  • Open Library, when you scan a barcode
  • Google Play, to buy or restore Pro
  • Export to CSV or JSON any time
  • Works with Android backup and transfer
