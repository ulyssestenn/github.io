Notes

The phones use placeholder rectangles so the focus stays on the accent. Once you pick a blue I'll wire ShelfScan's real screenshots into apps.html.

The accent color drives: the ::before rule on .eyebrow, the italic <em> color in the title, the chip border (slightly tinted), the .secondary-link underline, the .stat__value color, and the radial .app-row__glow behind the phones.
