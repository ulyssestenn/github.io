About Ulix

Ulix is an independent software company founded and run by Bethany Hunt.

It makes focused applications for readers, collectors, researchers, and other people who work with books, text, notes, and records.

Apps

Guten Read public-domain books offline, with highlights, notes, and export tools. → Shelf Scan Search your physical bookshelves with the camera. → Keep Clip Save text and links from almost any app, then search, organize, and export them. → Breaker of Horses Read the Iliad and Odyssey in Greek and English, separately or side by side. → Track Analysis Log observations in plain English and export the results as structured data. → Loan It Keep track of what you have lent, who has it, and when it should come back. → Curious Air Examine the wireless, network, and device signals in the world around you. → Scepter Turn screenshots into clean, tagged, searchable cards automatically, on-device. →
