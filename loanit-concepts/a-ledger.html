Books

Scan the barcode, skip the typing

Books are the thing people lose track of most. Scan an ISBN and Loan It fills in the title, author, publisher, and page count from Open Library before the book is out the door.

  • Three lookups free, unlimited with Pro
  • Only the ISBN leaves your phone
Photos

Photograph the item as you hand it over. When a box of computer parts comes back, you have a picture of what was in it — not a memory of what you think was in it.

  • One photo free, unlimited with Pro

Borrowers

Some people are worth
a gentle reminder.

Every borrower has a page: what they have now, what they have had before, and how long they tend to keep it. Free shows your top three overdue borrowers. Pro opens the full set of insights.

  • Most overdue borrowers, ranked
  • Average loan duration per person
  • Full lending history, still searchable after it is returned

Your lending history is nobody else's business

Loan records and photos are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Loan It reaches the network in exactly two places, and both are listed here.

  • No account to create
  • No ads, no third-party analytics
  • Open Library, only when you scan a barcode
  • Google Play, only to buy or restore Pro
  • Export to CSV or JSON whenever you want
  • Included in Android backup and device transfer
