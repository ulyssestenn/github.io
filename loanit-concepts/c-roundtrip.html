Getting it on the record takes seconds

Add the item, take a photo or scan a book's barcode, choose the borrower, and set the day it should come back — or let the default due date fill it in.

  • Gallery, camera, or ISBN barcode
  • One contact at a time, through Android's picker
  • A due date that cannot land before the loan did
Back

Closing a loan takes one

Mark it returned from the list, the borrower's page, or the loan itself. Undo sits right there if you close the wrong one, and returned loans stay searchable.

  • One Mark Returned button, three places to press it
  • Full history for every borrower
  • Overdue-borrower insights
