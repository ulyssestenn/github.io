What it looks like

Three screens, and you have the whole app

When it leaves

Write it down once

Item, photo, borrower, and a due date. Pick the borrower through Android's own contact picker — Loan It never reads your whole address book.

While it is out

Find it in a second

Search by item or name. Filter by overdue, due soon, outstanding, or returned. Sort by due date to see what is coming back next.

When it comes home

One tap, and it is closed

Mark it returned from the list, the borrower's page, or the loan itself. Undo is there if you tapped the wrong row.

Books

Scan the barcode and the record writes itself

Books go out the door faster than anything else. Scan an ISBN and Loan It fills in the title, author, publisher, and page count from Open Library, so there is nothing to type.

  • Three lookups free, unlimited with Pro
  • Nothing about you is sent — only the ISBN
Photos

A picture of what actually went out

Photograph the item as you hand it over. When a box of parts comes back, you can check it against what left, instead of against what you remember.

  • One photo free, unlimited with Pro
  • Photos stay in the app's private storage
Privacy

Your records stay on your phone

Loans and photos live in the app's private storage. There is no account to create and no analytics or tracking SDKs in the app. Loan It touches the network in exactly two places.

  • Open Library, when you scan a barcode
  • Google Play, to buy or restore Pro
  • Export to CSV or JSON any time
  • Works with Android backup and transfer
