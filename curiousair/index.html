The invisible world, on one screen

One console. Every signal.

Curious Air is a set of live instruments built on the radios and sensors already in your phone.

Wi-Fi Explorer

Every network in range, with live signal strength, channel, frequency, and security details.

Bluetooth Radar

Nearby BLE and classic devices with decoded advertisements and manufacturer names.

GNSS Sky Map

The satellites overhead right now — constellation, signal, and your live position accuracy.

Cellular

Cell identity, band, and signal readings, where your device and Android version allow.

NFC Reader

Tap a tag to inspect it: tag type, technology, and decoded NDEF payloads.

Magnetic Field

Live magnetometer readings and compass heading. Hunt for anomalies and hidden magnets.

Sound & Spectrum

Live sound levels and an audio spectrum view, up into the ultrasonic range.

Motion & Environment

Accelerometer, orientation and tilt, barometric altitude, and ambient light — all live.

Plus experimental instruments: inferred signals like 60 Hz AC-power fields, and camera-based signal tools.

Bluetooth Radar

Bluetooth, actually decoded.

Most scanners show you hex. Curious Air decodes BLE advertisements field by field, names manufacturers from the official Bluetooth SIG database, and lets you connect to read live GATT values in plain language.
