FAQ

Project Gutenberg on Android, answered.

Is there a Project Gutenberg reader for Android?

Guten is an independent Android ebook reader designed around Project Gutenberg books. It lets you search the catalog, download books, organize a library, and read with themes, bookmarks, highlights, notes, and full-text search.

Can I read Project Gutenberg books offline on Android?

Yes. Download a book in Guten first and it remains available for offline reading.

Can I open a Gutenberg.org book link in Guten?

Yes. Share a supported Project Gutenberg book link to Guten for import, or enter the Project Gutenberg ebook number in Guten’s import tool.

Is Guten free?

Yes. The core reading experience is free, including book discovery and downloads, offline reading, bookmarks, highlights, notes, in-book search, themes, reading goals, backup and restore, and Basic Share Studio. Optional Premium is a one-time purchase for additional tools.

Does Guten require an account?

No. Guten does not require an account to read or manage your library.

Is Guten made by Project Gutenberg?

No. Guten is an independent app from Ulix LLC. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to Project Gutenberg or the Project Gutenberg Literary Archive Foundation.
