What we believe

Built on products, not data.

One promise across every Ulix app. Local-first by default. Sustained by people who like the work, not by selling what you do inside it.

i

Privacy first

Local-first architecture with offline capability. No ads, tracking, or unnecessary accounts. Permissions only when essential.

ii

No subscriptions

Free basics stay free. When something is worth paying for, it’s a one-time purchase — not a treadmill.

iii

Focused tools

Clean, purposeful interfaces that enhance your day without demanding constant attention. No engagement loops.
